How It Works
Every OpenGradient inference leaves a permanent on-chain trail
01
You Run Inference
You call the OpenGradient SDK or x402 API. Your request goes to a TEE-verified inference node.
02
Proof Settled On-Chain
After inference, a cryptographic proof is settled on the OpenGradient blockchain. Immutable, permanent.
03
Generate Your Receipt
Paste your transaction hash here. Get a beautiful receipt proving exactly what ran, when, and how.
